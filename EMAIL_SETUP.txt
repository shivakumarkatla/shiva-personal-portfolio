EMAIL SETUP

The contact email lives in one place: src/data/profile.js

  const email = "katlashivakumar2003@gmail.com";
  const emailSubject = "Portfolio enquiry";

Every email link is built from these values:
- mailtoUrl: "Email me" buttons (homepage contact section, page-end
  contact panels) and the footer email icon. Opens the visitor's default
  email app with the subject prefilled.
- emailComposeUrl: the "write in Gmail instead" fallback link, which opens
  Gmail's web compose window in a new tab with recipient and subject filled.
- The Copy button (src/components/ui/CopyEmail.jsx) copies the address
  and announces "Email address copied" (or a failure message).

Note: a mailto: link does not send email. It hands the address to the
email app configured on the visitor's device. If no app is configured (or
the configured app is broken, e.g. the retired Windows Mail app), nothing
opens. That is why the Gmail and copy options are shown next to the button.
No contact form or third-party service is used.
